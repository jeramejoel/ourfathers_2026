The second EP.

Revival. Reprisal. Recompense.

Between hell and purgatory, Virgil sends him a dream. He wakes inside it, in a church that is burning and wants him back. Four songs: he wakes, he kneels, he joins the fire, and something takes root inside him. When the dream ends, he is on the shore of the mountain where Faith Healer begins.

Movements II, the second EP from ourfathers., evokes a new voice emerging from the ichor of faith. Driven by loss, conviction, and self-depravity, the new release explores philosophies of self-importance and the deluge of compromises we make to accept ourselves.

The debut LP.

funeral pyre follows Dante’s first journey, down through hell. A son loses his father to the sea and goes down after him, one circle at a time, to find him.

Twelve songs: nine circles, and three Furies who meet him on the way down, at the gate, at the walls of the burning city, and at the edge of the ice. With every circle he sees more of his father in himself. It ends at the bottom of hell, frozen and upside down, still asking the same question.

Faith Healer is the climb back out.

Recorded at the legendary Tiny Telephone SF (RIP), followed by a West Coast tour. 200 copies on double vinyl: coke-bottle green with white and oxblood splatter, gatefold, 12-page art book.

the long one

Born from grief and necessity, ourfathers. formed in 2014: a gathering of friends who found the only language left to honor their fathers. Song. Not to replace what was lost, but to build something that might survive it.

Drawing from the restless urgency of Converge, the atmosphere of Sigur Rós, the introspection of Mew, and the volatility of At The Drive-In, the band forged a sound both fragile and volatile: rooted in alternative, post-rock, punk, hardcore, and emo, but refusing to be easily named.

Their 2015 debut EP, Movements (recorded with Dryw Owens of From Indian Lakes and A Lot Like Birds), carved their first marks into the map, leading them to share stages with King Woman, State Faults, Hail the Sun, Wolf and Bear, and Mom Jeans, in rooms like Bottom of the Hill, Brick & Mortar, Thee Parkside, Hotel Utah, Neck of the Woods, the late Hemlock Tavern, Red House in Walnut Creek, and Homestead Bowl in Cupertino. In 2018 they independently released their first full-length, Funeral Pyre, recorded at the legendary Tiny Telephone SF (RIP), followed by a West Coast tour north to Portland and Olympia. Then the world staggered and fell silent, and in that silence ourfathers. returned with Movements II in 2021: a raw, unguarded testament shaped by distance, loss, and the ache of suspended time.

Now, after years of upheaval and transformation, and back on Bay Area stages at the Knockout, the Ivy Room, and Eli’s Mile High Club, ourfathers. emerge with their most vital work yet. Faith Healer is a fully self-produced album, recorded in their private Oakland studio: a meditation on loss, purgatory, memory, and fractured belief. A testament to what they have carried, what they have survived, and what they refuse to let be forgotten. Built on heavier progressive structures, experimental textures, and a sharpened emotional core, it speaks to anyone who has ever rebuilt a life out of ruins.

ourfathers. make music not for scenes or trends, but to mark what endures when all else is stripped away.
